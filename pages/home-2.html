01Featured project

One island. Three zones. Six aisles of attention.

Perspective render

  1. 01

    The brief

    NovaTech launched a new edge-computing line and needed one stand that worked for press briefings, live demos and quiet one-to-one meetings, all without a back wall to hide storage or hospitality.

    Client: NovaTech Systems

  2. 02

    The concept

    We split the 1,200 sq ft footprint into three zones around a monolithic product tower: a demo perimeter, a briefing counter cluster and a hidden meeting pod. A 12 m ring truss carries the brand at eye-level from every direction.

    Footprint: 1,200 sq ft

  3. 03

    The build

    Prefabricated in 14 modules at our Chicago workshop and pre-assembled once for a full dry-fit before shipping. Six-person crew, 3 days on site including electrical sign-off; dismantle in 14 hours.

    Crew: 6 · 3 days

  4. 04

    The result

    The stand delivered 1,900+ badge scans, 62 booked meetings and a hall inspection with nothing to fix.

    Results reported by client
