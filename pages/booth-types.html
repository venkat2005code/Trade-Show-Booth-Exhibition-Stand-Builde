Booth types

Find the footprint that fits your show.

Eight booth configurations, from a lean 10 × 10 inline to a two-level pavilion. Compare open sides, size ranges, strengths and customisation options.

Request a Project QuoteView Pricing

Booth types in detail

  1. 4 open sides20 × 20 to 60 × 60 ft

    Island Booths

    Ideal use
    Brands that want maximum visibility and a 360° experience in the middle of the hall.
    Typical layout
    Free-standing footprint with aisles on all four sides, no shared walls.

    Benefits

    • Visible from every direction
    • Room for demos, meeting areas and hospitality
    • Hanging signs allowed in most halls

    Customisation options

    • Ring or grid truss overhead
    • Two-level meeting rooms
    • Interactive product zones
    Quote this boothSee projects

  2. 3 open sides20 × 20 to 40 × 40 ft

    Peninsula Booths

    Ideal use
    Exhibitors who want strong exposure with a back wall for storage, graphics and screens.
    Typical layout
    Attached to a neighbour at the back with aisles on three sides.

    Benefits

    • Excellent aisle exposure
    • Hidden storage and hospitality behind the wall
    • Good balance of budget and impact

    Customisation options

    • Full-height feature wall
    • Corner reception counter
    • Private meeting room
    Quote this boothSee projects

  3. 1 open side10 × 10 to 10 × 30 ft

    Inline Booths

    Ideal use
    First-time exhibitors and lean teams who need a professional presence on a defined budget.
    Typical layout
    In a row between neighbours with one aisle-facing front.

    Benefits

    • Lower cost per show
    • Easy to install and transport
    • Simple, focused message

    Customisation options

    • Custom counter and fascia
    • Backlit graphics
    • Product plinths and shelving
    Quote this boothSee projects

  4. 2 open sides10 × 10 to 20 × 20 ft

    Corner Booths

    Ideal use
    Brands wanting more exposure than an inline for only a small step up in cost.
    Typical layout
    At the end of a row with aisles on two adjacent sides.

    Benefits

    • Two-sided visibility
    • Angled sightlines from two aisles
    • Efficient use of floor area

    Customisation options

    • Angled reception
    • Wraparound graphic wall
    • Small demo station
    Quote this boothSee projects

  5. 1 - 4 open sides10 × 10 to 30 × 30 ft

    Modular Booths

    Ideal use
    Teams that exhibit at multiple shows and need one system that reconfigures for any footprint.
    Typical layout
    Standard panel and frame grid reconfigurable per show.

    Benefits

    • Reusable across shows
    • Fast installation
    • Lower total cost over time

    Customisation options

    • Custom fascia and counter
    • Interchangeable graphics
    • Add-on meeting rooms
    Quote this boothSee projects

  6. 2 levels30 × 30 to 60 × 40 ft

    Double-Decker Stands

    Ideal use
    Large exhibitors who need private hospitality space without giving up ground floor exposure.
    Typical layout
    Structural upper deck with stairs and a glazed balustrade.

    Benefits

    • Doubles usable area
    • Private meeting and hospitality space
    • Landmark presence in the hall

    Customisation options

    • Glazed lounge
    • Feature staircase
    • Roof terrace or sign
    Quote this boothSee projects

  7. Multi-exhibitor40 × 30 ft and above

    Custom Pavilions

    Ideal use
    Trade bodies, regions and consortia who need one identity over many exhibitors.
    Typical layout
    Shared structure with individual bays and a communal hub.

    Benefits

    • One identity for many exhibitors
    • Shared cost, shared power
    • Flexible bay sizes

    Customisation options

    • Central hub
    • Shared fascia and gateway
    • Per-exhibitor styling
    Quote this boothSee projects

  8. Any layout10 × 10 to 40 × 40 ft

    Rental Stands

    Ideal use
    Teams who want a professional stand for a single show, or trial a footprint before investing.
    Typical layout
    Pre-engineered kit in any footprint, finished with custom graphics.

    Benefits

    • Lower up-front cost
    • Shorter lead time
    • No storage or refurbishment

    Customisation options

    • Custom graphics
    • Rental counters and furniture
    • Extra lighting
    Quote this boothSee projects
