# AI Image Prompts

Base style for every prompt: *photorealistic, editorial commercial photography for an exhibition stand and trade show booth design, fabrication and installation studio; natural or soft studio light; authentic real-world setting; realistic materials and textures; no text, watermarks or brand logos.* Show real workshops, show floors and event halls; any stand graphics should be blank or fictional (no real exhibitor brands).
